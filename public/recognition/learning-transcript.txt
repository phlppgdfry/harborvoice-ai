Reliable multilingual voice agents are powerful tools, but they have limitations. It's crucial to distinguish what the agent knows from what it guesses. Always preserve human approval for critical decisions. For example, if a voice agent says your fictional shipment is on customs hold and it can release it, that's a guess. It cannot release a shipment. Its function is to provide information and facilitate, not to act as an authority on external systems. A key limitation is its inability to verify real-world status without direct integrated access. Exercise. Design a simple voice agent interaction for ordering food. Identify one piece of information the agent would definitively know and one piece it would need to verify with the user or an external system
