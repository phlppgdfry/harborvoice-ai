Music generated in the native UI is publishable; music generated by a Flow's music node does land in History. No supported import, link or migration route was found in the documentation or the interface. This is an observed limitation, not a documented rule: the provider has not said that API-generated music is ineligible.

No private endpoint was called and no publishing request was reverse-engineered. Publish was not clicked on any song, including the unrelated "Summer Never Gets Old" that appeared in History on 10 November.

## Listening checklist for the owner

Listen to each track once on headphones and once on laptop speakers, start to finish.

1. **Opening.** Does it start cleanly? For Northern Lights, is the first second and a half a fade-in or dead air?
2. **Section joins** at about 0:20 and 0:40. Any audible seam, tempo jump or key change?
3. **Ending.** Does it resolve, or is it cut off? Is the tail long enough?
4. **Artefacts.** Any warble, metallic shimmer, clicks or distortion in the loudest passage?
5. **Fit.** Would it sit under a narrator without fighting the voice?
6. **Identity.** Does it resemble a specific existing song or artist closely enough to worry you?
7. **Title, description and cover.** Do they match what you hear?

Record a yes or no per track with the date. Nothing has been approved by listening so far.
