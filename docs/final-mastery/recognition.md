# Recognition and program review

Official sources checked 9 November 2026; cached source timestamps are preserved in [research metadata](research-sources.json). No recognition award, invitation, application acceptance, customer, follower count or participant is established by this audit.

[Ambassador terms](https://elevenlabs.io/ambassador-terms), dated 9 October 2026, distinguish Community Builders, Creator Partners and invitation-only Power Users. Applicants must satisfy the current eligibility rules; selection is discretionary. Owner must review eligibility, obligations and any identity/payment declarations. No amount of credit consumption guarantees an invitation, and artificial metric inflation is prohibited. This is not an exam certification.

[Ambassador](https://elevenlabs.io/ambassador) and [Creator Partner](https://elevenlabs.io/cpp) currently link to the same official application form. Treat the prepared answers as related tracks, not proof of two independent approvals. The [existing application](../recognition/applications.md) can now add the versioned AgentOps receipt importer, structured EN/NL/FR contracts, static AgentProof explorer and EPUBCheck result. These are engineering/educational artifacts, not adoption evidence. Community plans remain proposed.

## Owner-controlled LinkedIn connection

1. Sign in to the correct ElevenLabs account and open [Connected Apps](https://elevenlabs.io/app/settings/connected-apps).
2. Inspect LinkedIn's displayed connection/eligibility state and the capabilities the provider proposes to share. The last account-specific observation is 7 November; Phase 4 native browser transport is unavailable.
3. If Connect is offered, personally complete the LinkedIn consent flow for the intended LinkedIn account. Review actual requested permissions. Do not share login credentials.
4. Inspect the resulting LinkedIn capability statement and any separately displayed Top User status. Record only what appears; capability sharing is not automatically Top User recognition.
5. If blocked, retain the exact provider message and ask through official support after choosing to contact them. Public qualifying thresholds and refresh cadence remain unverified.

The [LinkedIn help reference](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a9616064) is linked in the historical research; general search did not establish new ElevenLabs-specific thresholds. This audit did not connect or inspect the user's LinkedIn account.
