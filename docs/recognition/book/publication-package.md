## Publication description

How do you build a voice assistant that preserves the difference between a checked fact and an authorized decision? This practical engineering book follows a fictional logistics assistant from business rules through knowledge retrieval, safe tools, multilingual behavior, evaluation, budget controls and deployment. It explains concrete failures, the difference between software tests and provider simulations, and the limits of the evidence collected. Readers finish with a reusable review protocol and exercises for their own voice-agent projects.
