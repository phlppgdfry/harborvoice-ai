# Belgian community plan

Proposed location: Ghent first, Bruges alternative. [Hackerspace Gent](https://hackerspace.gent/) is an existing local technical community with a public wiki and contact route, verified 8 November 2026. Its suitability for this specific workshop, room availability, accessibility, audience, capacity and costs are unconfirmed. Research candidate only; no affiliation, contact or booking. Consult its community process before proposing an event. A second option is an owner-arranged educational/coworking room in Bruges; no particular venue is claimed available.

Planning schedule relative to approval: week 1 owner reviews content and privacy/contact route; week 2 asks venue/community and recruits 3–5 voluntary pilot developers; week 3 runs small usability pilot and revises materials; week 4 confirms date/capacity and publishes invitation after approval. These are proposed milestones, not appointments.

Capacity concept: 10–15 learners in pairs, instructor plus optional volunteer facilitator. Required provider budget: 0 for the complete local path. Venue/refreshments/travel: request actual quotes after approval; no invented prices or commitments. Optional live participant exercises are self-funded, capped and separately explained from the no-key core.

Accessibility questions before booking: step-free access, toilets, hearing/visual support, projector readability, seating, reliable power and quiet space. Ask for feedback without recording people. No sponsor logo or official branding permission is assumed.
